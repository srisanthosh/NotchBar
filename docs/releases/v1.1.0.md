An Activity tab, a smarter Up Next, and a close button on every banner.

## What's new

- **Activity tab.** CPU, memory pressure and swap, network and disk rates, and the apps using the most CPU, with their icons. A compact version sits on Home, and **Open Activity Monitor** is one click away. It only measures while you're looking.
- **Up Next, fixed.** Canceled meetings no longer show up. Click an event to open it in Calendar, or right-click for **Remind Me** and **Hide from Up Next**. On Home, Join is a compact button so the time and place aren't cut off.
- **Dismiss any banner.** Meeting reminders always have a close button beside Snooze and Join. Now-playing and headphone banners show one when you hover. A reminder that arrives while the notch is open appears as a line at the bottom instead of being lost.
- **Hide from Screenshots.** **… > Hide from Screenshots** leaves the notch out of screenshots, screen recordings and shared screens.
- **Clear the clipboard.** A **Clear** button on the Clipboard tab. If you have pinned items, it asks whether to keep them.
- **Tab bar.** The tabs now stay clear of the camera, whatever your notch size and however many tabs you show.

## Install

You need a MacBook with a notch and macOS 14 or later.

1. Download **NotchBar-v1.1.0.zip** below and open it.
2. Drag **NotchBar** into your **Applications** folder, replacing the old one.
3. Open NotchBar. macOS will say it can't verify the app, because NotchBar isn't notarized by Apple. Click **Done**.
4. Open **System Settings › Privacy & Security**, scroll to **Security**, and click **Open Anyway** next to NotchBar. Confirm with your password.

Your tab choices and settings carry over, and the new Activity tab is on by default. Hide it from **… > Show Tabs** if you don't want it.

## Good to know

- The app is built by GitHub Actions from this release's source code. To check your download, compare its SHA-256 with the `.sha256` file below: `shasum -a 256 NotchBar-v1.1.0.zip`.
- It isn't signed with an Apple Developer ID, so macOS treats each update as a new app. You may need to allow it again in **Privacy & Security**, and grant Accessibility again if you use **Replace System HUD**.
