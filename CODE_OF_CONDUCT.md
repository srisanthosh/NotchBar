# Code of Conduct

NotchBar is built by people with different backgrounds, experience levels and opinions. We want everyone who takes part, whether in issues, pull requests or discussions, to feel welcome and respected.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers. Everyone was new once.
- Assume good intentions, and ask before assuming the worst.
- Give feedback about the work, not the person. Explain why, and suggest a way forward.
- Accept feedback gracefully, and be willing to change your mind.
- Respect people's time. Keep discussions on topic.

## What isn't acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Discriminatory jokes or language about anyone's identity or background.
- Sexualised language or imagery.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Trolling, deliberately derailing discussions, or repeatedly pushing after being asked to stop.

## Where this applies

This code applies in all of NotchBar's spaces on GitHub, and wherever someone represents the project in public.

## Reporting a problem

If someone's behaviour makes you uncomfortable, contact the maintainer, [@srisanthosh](https://github.com/srisanthosh), on GitHub. You can also use GitHub's [report content](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam) feature. Reports are kept confidential.

## What happens next

The maintainer will review every report and respond as fairly as possible. Depending on what happened, that may mean a private conversation, a public warning, or removing comments, commits or people from the project.
